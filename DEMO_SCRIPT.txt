NIVESHRAKSHAK — 90 SECOND DEMO

1. Open the dashboard.
2. Say: “A user receives a financial message promising unusually high returns.”
3. Click Quick Demo → Suspicious investment.
4. Click Analyze Now.
5. Show the score and explain the indicators: guaranteed return, urgency and payment pressure.
6. Switch to Screenshot.
7. Upload the supplied WhatsApp-style screenshot.
8. Click Extract Text & Analyze.
9. Point to OCR Extracted Text.
10. Point to the same risk analysis generated from OCR output.
11. Open Recent Scans and explain that the backend database stores anonymous scan metadata.
12. Mention the privacy design: screenshots are processed locally in the browser and are not uploaded.
13. End with: “NiveshRakshak does not tell people what to invest in. It helps them pause, understand and verify before acting.”
